### Hi, I'm William 👋

I'm **William Zhiyuan Qi (齐智源)**, an undergraduate researcher at **Duke Kunshan University**, studying Applied Mathematics and Computation with a track in Computer Science.

- 🧠 **Perception:** multimodal learning, semantic-region attention and resource-aware model design.
- 🌀 **Prediction:** typhoon trajectories, spatiotemporal modeling and predictive uncertainty.
- 🌱 **Impact:** making research reproducible and environmental knowledge accessible through code.
